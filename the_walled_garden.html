The Walled Garden

Surrounded by ancient woodland and weathered by time, our two-acre Victorian walled garden sets the scene for a truly unique Norfolk wedding. Whether you choose a classic marquee or a stretch tent reception, thanks to our beautiful location we promise you a day to remember.

Enquire

Following your open air garden ceremony step into our cosy restored wooden guard’s van to sign the register as guests soak up the atmosphere or try their hand at croquet on the lawn.

Gather with friends and family to dine under canvas before the stunning Norfolk sunset appears to the west. As darkness falls, coloured lights bathe the walls and trees and, as you dance the night away under the stars, you'll see this historic garden in a whole new light. From dawn to dusk, High House Garden offers a taste of elegance in the wilderness - a blank canvas wedding venue which feels uniquely yours.
