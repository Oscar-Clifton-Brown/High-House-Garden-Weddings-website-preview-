The Venue

Take a step back in time and experience the magic of High House Gardens – an exclusive wedding venue in the East of England offering a truly romantic location for the wild at heart.

Enquire

Set in 50 acres of private parkland, this atmospheric Victorian estate is exclusively yours for the duration of your stay.

Just a stone’s throw from Sandringham, we offer a country wedding weekend to remember complete with open fires, woodland walks and even rowing on your very own lake.

Enquiry Brochure Book a visit

From the smoking fires of the courtyard buildings to the starry skies above our Victorian ‘kissing trees’ we guarantee you and your party a Norfolk wedding to remember. The sky really is the limit here for you to celebrate your love story in your own colourful way.

Our family has loved this place for generations, now fully licenced for civil weddings with onsite parking for more than 100 cars, we are delighted to be sharing it with you.
