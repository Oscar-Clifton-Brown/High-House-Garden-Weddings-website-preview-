Accommodation

As part of our three-day exclusive use wedding venue package we offer onsite accommodation in our two beautifully restored cottages, between them comfortably hosting up to 15 people

Grooms Cottage

Sleeping eight guests in four generous bedrooms, this traditional Carr Stone built property has been lovingly converted from a large Victorian stable to retain original character.. Thanks to the large kitchen, open fireplace and a picture window to die for, Grooms will no doubt become the hub of your wedding party preparation. And the vast master bedroom really has to be seen to be believed.

Stable Cottage

Stable Cottage is our converted Victorian carriage house. Sleeping seven, Stable has an open plan kitchen, living and dining room overlooking the old stable courtyard. Upstairs three large rooms catch the best of the evening light. Our master bedroom overlooks the Walled Garden, the ideal vantage point from which to watch guests gather and feel the excitement building.
