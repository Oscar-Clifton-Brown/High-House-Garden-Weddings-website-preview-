About us

Passed down the generations, the stables and coach house now offer attractive accommodation and the once crumbling walls of the garden have been brought back to their former glory by Cedric’s grandchildren Douglas and Philippe with the support of local craftsmen.

Originally built in 1865 for the Victorian painter and traveller Robert Elwes, High House was once a grand family home. Following a fire in 1939 the large house was destroyed, but almost exactly 100 years after it was first built, what survived of the High House estate and surrounding land was bought by our Grandfather Cedric Clifton Brown.

Today, a fourth generation of Clifton Browns are running wild in the woods and gardens. High House means the world to us – we are so excited to be sharing this special place with you.
